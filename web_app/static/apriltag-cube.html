AprilTag Cube Generator

Detect the printed tags with your phone's camera: AprilTag Detector (tag16h5, with pose and distance).

One ID, or several comma-separated IDs: one cube per ID, in the smallest square grid (2x2, 3x3, ...) with 2 mm spacing.
Six comma-separated IDs for faces 1-6. Faces 1, 2, 3 point along +X, +Y, +Z (right-hand rule); 4, 5, 6 are opposite to 3, 2, 1.
The tag (6 cells wide) sits inside a 1-cell white margin, so tag size = 0.75 x cube size. The STEP file contains two bodies, "white" (cube with pockets) and "black" (tag cells), for multimaterial printing. With axes enabled, "red" (+X) and "green" (+Y) arrows lie flat on the floor beside the cube, on its X and Y axes.
