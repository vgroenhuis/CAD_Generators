Sanding Disc Rack Generator

The count is the number of discs; compartments are sized for the stack plus some room to grab a disc. Grits are placed left to right in this order.
Enter grits and click Generate.
The rack hangs on Multiboard with Multiconnect connectors (25 mm grid). Discs stand on edge in a curved cradle that matches their size, so they cannot roll out; the cradle is 5 mm less deep than the front lip is high. Dividers drop into floor and back grooves and can be moved whenever a stack shrinks or grows. Grit labels clip onto the front lip and slide along it. The 3MF zip holds the tray, the divider, a sheet with all labels and a slot test piece, ready for the slicer; the STEP zip also has the assembly, for editing in CAD: print the test piece first to check the fit, and adjust the slot scale if needed. Print the tray upright, the divider flat and the labels on their side.
